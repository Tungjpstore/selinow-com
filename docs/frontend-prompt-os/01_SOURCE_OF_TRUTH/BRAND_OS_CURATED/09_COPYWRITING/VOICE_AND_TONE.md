# Voice and Tone

Selinow sounds clear, capable, calm, progressive, trustworthy, and human.

## Voice remains stable
- Direct, not abrupt.
- Confident, not arrogant.
- Helpful, not patronizing.
- Modern, not trend-chasing.

## Tone changes by context
- Marketing: optimistic and specific.
- Setup: encouraging and instructional.
- Payments: precise and reassuring.
- Errors: accountable and recovery-focused.
- Security: calm, factual, and explicit.
