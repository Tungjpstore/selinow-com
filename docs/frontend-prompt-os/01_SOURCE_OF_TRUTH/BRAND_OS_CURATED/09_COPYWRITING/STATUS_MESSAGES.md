# Status Messages

- Payment pending: `We’re waiting for payment confirmation. Do not pay again.`
- Payment verified: `Payment confirmed.`
- Fulfillment processing: `Your order is confirmed. Delivery is being prepared.`
- Fulfilled: `Your order is ready.`
- Payment failed: `The payment was not completed. No confirmed charge was recorded by Selinow.`
- Delivery failed: `Payment is confirmed, but delivery needs attention. Your order is safe and support has the reference.`
