# Brand Boundaries

What Selinow is and is not.

## Selinow is

A software platform for building and operating conversational commerce.

## Selinow is not

A bank, guaranteed payment processor, marketplace of record, or promise of earnings.

## Telegram-first

Telegram is the initial native channel, but the master brand should remain suitable for future messaging channels.

## Risky categories

Product policy must define prohibited goods and enforce abuse handling independently of marketing ambition.
