# Core Personas

Practical personas for product and communication decisions.

## Solo seller — Minh

Needs to launch quickly, avoid manual payment checking, and deliver digital products automatically.

## Community operator — Anna

Needs recurring access control, member status visibility, and failed-payment recovery.

## Agency owner — Omar

Needs multi-client management, repeatable setup, roles, reporting, and white-label options.

## Buyer — Kai

Needs a fast checkout, clear merchant identity, immediate delivery, and a reliable way to get support.
