# Positioning

The market position Selinow should own.

## Positioning statement

For creators, digital sellers, and Telegram-native merchants who need to sell without assembling a fragile stack, Selinow is a conversational commerce platform that combines storefronts, payments, automation, and fulfillment in one system.

## Category shorthand

Telegram commerce platform; conversational commerce automation.

## Competitive frame

More commerce-native than chatbot builders, more automated than generic storefront tools, and easier to operate than custom bot development.

## Proof themes

Fast setup, reliable fulfillment, native Telegram experience, auditable workflows, and global edge infrastructure.
