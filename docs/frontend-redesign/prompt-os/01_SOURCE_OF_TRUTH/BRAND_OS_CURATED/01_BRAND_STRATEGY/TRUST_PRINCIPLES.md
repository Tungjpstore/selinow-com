# Trust Principles

Commerce surfaces must make trust visible.

## Identity

Show merchant identity and support path before payment.

## Consent

Explain recurring charges, access rules, and refund terms before confirmation.

## Traceability

Provide order IDs, timestamps, payment state, and fulfillment history.

## Recovery

Never end in a dead state; provide retry, contact, or escalation.

## Privacy

Collect the least information necessary and expose data only to authorized roles.
