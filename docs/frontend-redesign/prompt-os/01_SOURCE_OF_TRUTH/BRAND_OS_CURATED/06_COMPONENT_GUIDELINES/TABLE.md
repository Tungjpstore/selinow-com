## Rules
- Keep the primary identifier in the first column.
- Persist sort state in the URL for shareable views.
- Provide empty, loading, error, and partial-data states.
- On mobile, switch to priority columns or cards rather than unreadable compression.

## AI validation
- Uses official tokens.
- Includes keyboard and screen-reader behavior.
- Includes loading, empty, error, and disabled states where applicable.
